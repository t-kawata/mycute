# Release v0.24.168

## Summary
Implemented adult HELP offer policy and child consent policy pure judgment functions (M1.75-4) as the fourth ticket of the Child Support Villages / HELP Consensus milestone.

## What's Changed
- **feat(darvium):** Added 4 RFC-compliant pure judgment functions for HELP consensus: `should_offer_help` (§41B.6 Eq 41B-10), `child_need_score` (§41B.7 Eq 41B-12), `decide_help_offer` (§41B.7 Eq 41B-13), and `compute_offer_score_breakdown`
- **feat(darvium):** Added 6 new types: `AdultHelpOfferPolicy`, `ChildHelpAcceptancePolicy`, `OfferScoreBreakdown`, `OfferDecision`, `ChildDecision`
- **feat(darvium):** Extended `HelpRejectionReason` from 6 to 10 variants (added Unsafe, Irrelevant, Overloaded, ResourceExhausted)
- **feat(darvium):** Added 12 calibration constants for HELP offer/accept policy in `constants.rs`
- **test(darvium):** Added 8 invariant tests (T-1~T-8) and 3 observational tests (T-O1~T-O3, n=18,000 total samples)
- **docs(darvium):** Updated ticket M1.75-4 status to done in Darvium-Tickets-v2.3.md
